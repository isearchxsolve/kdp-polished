PART 0 — ONE-TIME ACCOUNT SETUP (do this before the first book)
=====================================================================
1. Go to kdp.amazon.com -> Sign up -> create account with your email.
2. Two-Step Verification: switch it ON (they will ask; phone SMS works).
3. Left menu -> "Complete your account" -> three tabs:

   TAB A — Author/publisher info:
     - Are you a publisher?        -> NO (you are an individual author)
     - Country                     -> India
     - Your name                   -> your REAL name here (never NILOY;
                                      the account is the payer, the
                                      books carry the pen name)
     - Address, phone              -> your real Assam address + phone

   TAB B — Getting paid (bank):
     - Bank country                -> India
     - Account holder              -> your real name
     - IFSC + account number       -> your real bank account
     - (Royalties arrive in INR; small minimum before payout.)

   TAB C — Tax information:
     - Are you a US person?        -> NO
     - Tax form                    -> W-8BEN (individual)
     - Country of citizenship      -> India
     - Treaty claim: India-US treaty -> withholding drops to 15%
       (without PAN it is 30%; enter PAN when asked)
     - Sign electronically, submit. Status should turn green "Valid".

PART 1 — UNDERSTANDING THE FORM (same 3 tabs for every book)
=====================================================================
KDP calls the form "Title Setup". It has 3 sections, top to bottom:

  [1] BOOK DETAILS   - title, subtitle, author, description,
                       keywords, categories, age/audience
  [2] BOOK CONTENT   - ISBN (skip - free KDP ISBN), upload file,
                       cover, preview, AI questions
  [3] RIGHTS & PRICING - territories, price, royalty, publish

RULES THAT APPLY TO ALL SEVEN BOOKS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Language:                 English
Publication rights:       "I own the copyright and hold necessary
                          publishing rights" (TRUE - you wrote them)
Adult content:            NO  (Book 05 handles dark themes in memoir
                          form; it is not explicit. Answer NO, but
                          select the Mental-Health-friendly categories
                          so it reaches the right readers.)
AI question - text:       "Did you use AI tools in creating texts...
                          ?" -> YES for ALL books (honest + safe).
                          Then pick: "AI-assisted" (you wrote, AI
                          helped edit/format) for books 01-05, 07.
                          Book 06 is "AI-generated" (Claude wrote it)
                          - its title page already says so.
AI question - images:     covers are typographic/vector art drawn by
                          code -> answer "AI-assisted" NO... if asked
                          whether AI created images, answer YES with
                          "AI-assisted" if your covers came from
                          KDP-POLISHED\COVERS (generated by script).
                          Honesty here costs nothing.
Territories:              "All territories (worldwide rights)" - YES
DRM:                      OFF (easier for readers, no downside for us)
Series page:              Books 01/02/03 -> same series name
                          "The Beholder Project" (creates a linked
                          series page on Amazon - do it, it is free
                          marketing). 04/05/06/07 -> companion books,
                          can also join the same series as "companions"
                          - simpler: only link 01-03 as the series.

PART 3 — CLICK-BY-CLICK FOR EACH BOOK (about 20 minutes)
=====================================================================
1.  Bookshelf -> + Create -> "Kindle eBook" (do eBooks first; add
    paperback later from the same title once eBook is live).
2.  [Book Details] Enter the Title/Subtitle exactly as in Part 2.
    Series: choose "The Beholder Project" for 01/02/03 (numbered
    1,2,3). Author: NILOY DAS (+ contributor Claude on book 06).
3.  Description: copy from Part 2. It accepts some HTML - plain
    paste is fine. No HTML needed.
4.  Keywords: paste the 7, one per box. Categories: pick the two
    listed (search the picker with the exact words).
5.  Age/audience: leave default (no age restriction). Pre-order: SKIP.
6.  [Book Content] ISBN: "Get a free KDP ISBN" (auto, do nothing).
    Upload manuscript: the .docx of that book.
    Upload cover: the matching .jpg from COVERS\.
7.  Launch Previewer - eyeball title page, contents, a few chapters.
    (Books have a built-in Contents page; Kindle also auto-creates
    its own from the headings. Both are fine.)
8.  AI questions: answer as in the RULES box (Part 1).
    Terms: check "I have read and agree".
9.  [Rights & Pricing] Territories: All. DRM: off.
    Price: from Part 2. Royalty plan: 70% (except book 04).
    KDP Select: SKIP for now (can join later; it demands 90-day
    exclusivity - decide calmly later, not today).
10. Click "Publish your Kindle eBook". Review takes up to 72 hours.
    Status becomes "Live" on the Bookshelf. Done. Next book.
